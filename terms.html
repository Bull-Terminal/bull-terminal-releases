Bull Terminal - terms of use and privacy

Last updated: October 25, 2026 (Bull Terminal 3.1)

  1. Not financial advice. Bull Terminal is a market scanner and research tool. It shows statistics computed from past prices and public data: scores, forecasts, odds, risk levels and back-tests. They are not recommendations to buy or sell anything and are not guaranteed to be accurate or complete. Past results do not predict future results. You alone decide what to trade, and you are responsible for your own trades and losses. Crypto and small stocks can lose most of their value quickly - only trade money you can afford to lose.
  2. No trading, no custody. The app never places orders and never holds money or crypto. Paper trading uses pretend money. "Trade it" buttons only open the exchange's or broker's own website.
  3. Data comes from third parties (CoinMarketCap, CoinGecko, crypto exchanges, Alpaca, nasdaq.com, CBOE, Hyperliquid, Kraken and others), using your own free keys where needed. Their data can be late, wrong or unavailable; their own terms apply to your use of them.
  4. Referral links. Some "Sign up" links in the app are referral links, marked "referral". If you open an account through one, the seller may earn a commission and you may get a sign-up bonus or fee discount. It costs you nothing extra and never changes scores or picks.
  5. License. Your purchase gives you a personal license to use Bull Terminal on one computer at a time (move it whenever you like). Don't resell, share or publish your license key or the app. Monthly plans stay active while the subscription is paid; a cancelled or refunded purchase turns Pro off (the Free plan keeps working).
  6. Refunds. Try Pro free for 14 days before buying. If Bull Terminal doesn't run on your Windows PC, or you're unhappy, ask for a refund within 14 days of purchase through the store you bought from (or the support email on the store page) - no hard questions. Monthly plans can be cancelled any time; you keep Pro until the paid month ends.
  7. No warranty. The app is provided "as is", without warranties of any kind. To the fullest extent the law allows, the seller is not liable for any loss or damage arising from using it, including trading losses.
  8. Privacy. Everything runs on your PC. There are no accounts, no tracking and no analytics. - What stays on your PC: your keys, watchlists, plans, trades, journal, results and settings. API keys and tokens are stored encrypted by Windows, so only your Windows account on that PC can read them. - What the app sends out, and nothing else: requests to the data sources above (prices, charts, futures data, coin logo images from CoinMarketCap / CoinGecko, which are then kept on your PC, and headlines from the public news feeds of Cointelegraph, CoinDesk, Decrypt and CryptoSlate - nothing about you is sent to them); your license key and a one-way hash of your PC's id to the license checker; your phone-alert messages (and the morning briefing, if you turn it on) to Discord / Telegram if you set them up; and a check for a newer version (an update - or going back to the version you had before - is only installed if it carries the seller's signature). - Feedback is sent only when you press Send in Report a problem / Suggest an idea / Vote: your message, the features you voted for, the app version, Windows version and plan (free / trial / pro), and an email address only if you type one. It goes to the seller's feedback inbox. No keys, logs, file paths or PC id are included. - Backups ("Back up everything") stay on your PC, in the data folder. They leave out your API keys. - Phone view is off unless you start it. Then a read-only page (today's picks, your positions) is reachable on your home network only, through a secret link, for up to 4 hours. Nothing is sent to the internet for it. - In the background: background scans run on a Windows schedule only if you turn them on (in the app or when installing), and only while you're signed in to Windows. Starting with Windows is also off unless you choose it. - Support files are made only when you press the button and are never sent automatically. They leave out your API keys, tokens, webhook links and your Windows user name. - Your data, your call: uninstalling keeps your data folder, so a reinstall picks up where you left off. Delete the Bull Terminal folder inside %LOCALAPPDATA% to remove everything.
  9. Changes. These terms can be updated with new versions of the app; the version included with your copy applies.
